## GPU numerical limits

The CUDA comparator retains its `atol + rtol × abs(package value)` rule for all fields except recovered velocity change. For `physical_changes.velocity` only, each float32 cell has a one-local-model-ULP floor: `m = max(abs(common velocity), abs(original final velocity), abs(package final velocity))`, `q = nextafter_float32(m, +∞) − m`, and the bound is `max(atol + rtol × abs(package change), q)`. CPU comparisons remain exact; full velocity, source/sigma, critic, gradients, Adam, schedulers/scalers and RNG retain their existing checks. In the failed 2,800-cell comparison, **539** cells failed the old bound, the floor rescued **17**, and **522 still failed**. This narrow resolution allowance did not make a material disagreement pass.

The pinned DeepWave 0.0.9 CUDA source uses shared-cell `atomicAdd` accumulation for model gradients across shot blocks. Its reduction order may vary despite cuDNN deterministic settings and restored RNG. Critic AMP or max-pool operations may amplify small changes, but **the cause of the observed divergence remains unproven**. A checkpoint restores registered physical/optimizer/scheduler/scaler state and random streams; it does not promise a bit-exact GPU trajectory.

The opt-in `f54f251` repeatability mode ran at `3b0b5d2` on tiny-clean from one calibrated common state: three two-epoch replays each under default precision and a separately labelled cuDNN-TF32-off ablation. All **12 branch starts** matched loaded physical/critic/optimizer/scheduler/scaler state and Python/NumPy/Torch/CUDA RNG; the common snapshot remained unchanged. Across both profiles, all **12 paired original/package epoch comparisons passed** the retained tolerance, while all **16 same-implementation replay comparisons failed** stricter bit-exact checks. Each of the 24 branch epochs recorded one physical and six critic optimizer updates, with zero work failures. The seed was already 42 and full RNG state was restored; no missing seed reset was observed. Disabling cuDNN TF32 did not restore bit-exact repeatability. See the [compact summary](gpu-repeatability-summary.json) and [compressed raw JSON](gpu-repeatability-diagnostic.json.gz) (`gzip -dc docs/validation/gpu-repeatability-diagnostic.json.gz`), plus the [measurement guide](../performance.md#cuda-measurement).

Most same-kind differences concern velocity gradients and Adam moments at roughly 1e-8 absolute scale. One default optimized self-replay also differs by one velocity ULP in one cell at epoch zero and ten cells at epoch one; epoch-one source, critic, source-gradient and source-Adam differences use different scales. The TF32-off profile's largest listed gradient difference is 2.2351742e-8. The profile's `comparison_failure_count = 8` counts **bit-exact self-comparisons**, not failed package tests or failed paired comparisons. This diagnostic is **untimed**. Its hooks copy tensors to CPU and synchronize execution, which may affect scheduling; passing the instrumented paired comparisons cannot retroactively pass the earlier uninstrumented 44-path material failure or establish GPU performance. Shared-cell native CUDA accumulation is compatible with variation, but the specific cause remains unproven.

## Audit and remaining work

All eight uniquely created package-validation Pods were deleted and their cleanup verified; [cleanup summary](pod-cleanup.json) records IDs and scope. Their combined **$0.537236** duration × reported-rate estimate excludes earlier legacy notebook tests and is **not a billing invoice**. Failed pre-create requests created no Pod and have no compute estimate.

Future acceptance of CUDA performance would require all workloads to pass numerical, successful-update and RNG gates with complete synchronized raw timing samples, native solver counts and absolute/incremental allocated/reserved peaks. Only then should `examples/plot_gpu_benchmark.py` render a chart. Further paid GPU optimization is deferred under the current delivery scope. Full Marmousi inversion/convergence, general GPU speedup and a modern DeepWave backend remain unverified. The [machine-readable summary](validation-summary.json) and [review decisions](review.md) preserve the evidence boundary.
