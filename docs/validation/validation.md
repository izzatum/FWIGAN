## Executable CPU workflow

The fresh full CPU suite at `7885070` completed **196 passed, 4 CUDA-dependent skipped, 11 upstream Matplotlib/Pyparsing warnings in 74.99 s**. The [log](final-cpu-tests.log) and [JUnit XML](final-cpu-tests.xml) are included with this record. The four skips are explicitly unrun CUDA tests on the CPU host. The repository's whole-branch review approved the delivered numerical, lifecycle, CLI, and documentation scope after the Marmousi source-coefficient correction ([review and decisions](review.md)).

The package CLI was exercised through ten real commands: tiny observation simulation; interrupted FWI inversion, resume, and evaluation; clean FWIGAN inversion, resume, and evaluation; and noisy FWIGAN inversion, resume, and evaluation. The [recorded outputs](task4-cli-evidence.json) are included. FWI reached epoch 24 with raw residual MSE **0.319536** and model RMSE **91.19294 m/s**. Clean/noisy FWIGAN reached epoch 2 after resume; these are smoke workflows, not method-comparison results. Both repository notebooks passed schema validation and executed all **three code cells each** with the package Python kernel; evidence is `/workspace/fwigan-runtime/FWIGAN-package-executed.ipynb` and `FWIGAN_Noisy-package-executed.ipynb` These executed notebook files are retained locally; the source notebooks remain reproducible clients in the repository.

The real bounded, noise-free DeepWave FWI illustration used a homogeneous 2100 m/s truth, a 2000 m/s initializer, and a **2000 m/s lower bound equal to that initializer**. Over 24 epochs, raw residual MSE fell **46.46508 → 0.319536**, and global model RMSE **100.0 → 91.19294 m/s**. The grids for truth and inversion are the same in this illustration, and recovery at depth remains incomplete. Numerical arrays, run metadata, and plots are under `docs/assets/`; their provenance is `examples/illustrate.py` with its included numeric archive and metadata.

The frozen-original-versus-package CPU benchmark used matched warmed state, six critic cycles, two steps per timing window, and three repetitions. Clean median time was **0.379336 → 0.171540 s per shot group**; noisy median **0.357333 → 0.173451 s**. Native instrumentation found **one solver forward and seven adjoints** for the original, versus **one forward and one adjoint** for the package, per group. Physical/critic/Adam/gradient and RNG comparisons passed under the recorded tolerances. These timings are tiny shared-host CPU evidence, not a GPU or full-scale speedup. Raw samples, code hashes, and method details are in `docs/assets/tiny-cpu-benchmark.json` and `docs/performance.md`.

The Marmousi known source on the 30 m inversion grid is calibrated by the exact `(3/30)^2 = 0.01` cell-area coefficient while the original 3 m fine-grid observation source and the FWIGAN 10 Hz initializer remain unchanged. A real interior, off-source native-solver probe measured a **100.3091** coarse/fine norm ratio with equal coefficients and **0.026225** relative L2 after the deterministic correction. This does not establish complete raw equivalence for product surface/colocated geometry or differing physical PML widths. Sources: `docs/validation/grid-source-calibration.md` and `.json`.
